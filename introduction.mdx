## How Righthand is organized

The platform has four primary team areas:

- **Activity** shows work and communications across the team.
- **Righthands** lists your assistants and opens each Righthand's profile.
- **Connections** manages accounts and services available to the team.
- **Team settings** manages members, domains, usage, budgets, and billing.

Each Righthand profile has four areas:

- **Activity** for that Righthand's work and communications.
- **Responsibilities** for the work they own.
- **Connections** for the accounts and services assigned to them.
- **Manage** for individual settings and integrations.
