The current hire flow has three stages: **Own**, **Meet**, and **Connect**. New Righthands start on the **Starter** plan at **$99 per month** with 1x usage capacity.

## Before you begin

You need a Righthand account and a payment method for your team. If no payment method is on file, the hire flow prompts you to add one before the Righthand is created.

## Hire a Righthand

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the hire flow">
    Go to [Hire a Righthand](https://teams.righthand.ai/righthands/new). You can also open the **Account** menu and select **Hire Righthand**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what they own">
    In **Own**, select one or more responsibility packs. A responsibility gives the Righthand a charter and may include skills or routines for ongoing work.

    Choose **Decide later** if you want to hire first and assign responsibilities afterward.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who you meet">
    In **Meet**, select one of the available profiles. Select **More** to see another group of profiles.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the offer">
    The offer summary shows the selected responsibility, the Righthand's name, and **You** as manager. New hires use the Starter plan; you can [change the plan](/guides/change-righthand-work-schedule) after hiring.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hire">
    Select **Hire**. Righthand claims the selected profile, creates the Righthand's phone number and email address, assigns the selected responsibilities, and begins setup.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect accounts">
    The flow moves to **Connect** while setup continues. Connect any accounts the Righthand will need, or open the profile and add connections later.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When setup finishes, select **Meet _name_** to open the new Righthand's profile. From there you can contact them, review Activity, assign responsibilities, manage connections, and change settings.
