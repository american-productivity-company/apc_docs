Each Righthand can have a Slack bot so people in your workspace can send direct messages and mention the Righthand.

## Connect Slack

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Righthand">
    Go to [Righthands](https://teams.righthand.ai/righthands) and select the Righthand you want to add to Slack.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Slack settings">
    Select **Manage**, then **Slack**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the integration">
    When the status is **Inactive**, select **Integrate**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize the correct workspace">
    Complete the Slack authorization flow. Confirm the workspace name and requested access before approving. A Slack administrator may need to approve the installation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the bot">
    Return to Righthand and confirm that the Slack status is **Active**. The pane shows the bot user and activation date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test a message">
    In Slack, send the Righthand a direct message or mention the Righthand in a channel where the bot is present. Confirm the response and corresponding Activity entry.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Communications policy

The Slack bot's installation and the Righthand's outbound Slack permission are separate controls. To restrict outward Slack messages, open **Manage → Communications Policy** and set the **Slack** channel to **Allow**, **Ask**, or **Never**.

## Deactivate Slack

Open **Manage → Slack**, select **Deactivate**, and confirm. Deactivation stops the bot from responding to direct messages and mentions. You can integrate it again later.

<Info>
  If Slack authorization succeeds but the bot does not respond, confirm that the bot is present in the conversation and that the Righthand is active. Then contact [support@humans.righthand.ai](mailto:support@humans.righthand.ai).
</Info>
